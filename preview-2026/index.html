What it does

Your portfolio, read every few minutes.

The hours watching the market and the work you'd otherwise do by hand.

No more screen time

You set the rules, and YILD reads the market against them all day with live prices. Let the market come to you.

No bookkeeping

YILD keeps your per-lot ledger, every sell and every buy with the right lot. LIFO cost - never your broker's blended average.

Dividend timing

YILD tracks every ex-date across your funds, so the buyback lands before the distribution, not after.

Working capital, not noise

Cost-basis working capital and realized income, the way income investors read it. Not market-value swings.

A discipline, made daily.

CEF income investing has a method: take profit when it's there, never miss a dividend, and manage working capital instead of chasing market value. Built for investors who follow the RMS method. YILD helps you run that method every day - without spreadsheets or manual bookkeeping. It turns the method into a clean operating layer for your own holdings, your own rules, and your own income strategy. Where most fintech adds complexity, YILD removes it: deliberate workflows, quiet interfaces, and zero noise.

Broker synced

Every morning, YILD pulls your holdings, trades, and distributions from your broker - the source of truth. Synced through the API you authorized.

Reconciled

YILD rebuilds your own per-lot ledger - positions, dividends, realized gains and working capital. True to cost - not average, not broker-restrained.

One-swipe

What crossed your thresholds shows up as a card. You see the full order and swipe once. That's it. Order sent to your own broker.

End-to-end execution

Every trade is tracked and the bookkeeping is done for you. Follow your capital gains and realized income as the day unfolds. Without the hassle.

Compliance

Secure by design.

YILD displays rule-based signals from the holdings, settings, and data you control - nothing more. YILD does not recommend securities and does not manage accounts. It sends only the orders you approve, one at a time, to your own broker. Your trading credentials never leave your machine. YILD never holds them. You authorize through your broker's own API and can revoke it at any time. YILD holds no funds and never takes custody of assets. You are a customer of your broker, not of YILD; YILD has no part in any transaction. No discretionary management, no standing orders, no broker scraping, no credential sharing, and no unsupported account access. Every decision, every trade, every data source, and every setting remains yours, with full transparency.
